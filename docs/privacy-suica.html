Privacy Policy — SquirrelSuica

Package: works.squirrel.suica · Developer: Squirrel Works · Last updated: 2026-11-01

SquirrelSuica is a read-only FeliCa helper. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • NFC / FeliCa card data is read and processed only on your device.
  • The app does not upload card balances, IDm, history, or station data to our servers.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no sale of personal data.

Data the app processes on-device

When you hold a compatible transit or e-money card to the phone, the app may read publicly documented FeliCa service data (for example balance and journey/purchase history) and show it in the UI. That information stays in app memory / local UI state on the device and is not transmitted by the app to Squirrel Works.

Permissions

The app uses NFC to communicate with FeliCa cards while it is open in the foreground. It does not require an account or network access for card reading.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, crash, or advertising SDKs that send user data off-device.
  • No account identifiers, email, or location collection by the app.
  • No sale or sharing of card data with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
