Privacy Policy — SquirrelSuicaAnalysis

Package: works.squirrel.suicaanalysis · Developer: Squirrel Works · Last updated: 2026-11-04

SquirrelSuicaAnalysis is a read-only FeliCa debug and analysis tool. It shows what a card answers so that card support in Suica Reader can be improved. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • NFC / FeliCa card data is read and processed only on your device.
  • The app does not upload card data to our servers. It has no network permission.
  • A dump is saved or leaves the app only when you tap Save JSON… or Share, and only to the place or app you pick.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no sale of personal data.

Data the app processes on-device

When you hold a compatible transit or e-money card to the phone, the app asks the card which systems and services it has, reads the services that need no key (for example balance and journey/purchase history), and records technical details such as response times and status flags. It also reads the card's IDm (card identifier) and PMm. The results are shown in the app and kept in app memory. The app does not save them by itself, and it never transmits them to Squirrel Works.

Saving and sharing a dump

You can export the results as a JSON dump in two ways. Save JSON… opens the Android file picker, and the app writes the file only to the location you choose (for example Downloads or a cloud drive folder); the app has no access to that file afterwards, and you can delete it like any other file. Share sends the dump to the app you choose in the Android share sheet (for example email or a messaging app), and from then on that app's privacy policy applies. A dump can contain card history and the card identifier. The Redact IDm in exports option removes the card identifier from both saved and shared dumps. If you send a dump to Squirrel Works to help improve card support, we use it only for that purpose and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Permissions

The app uses NFC to communicate with FeliCa cards while it is open in the foreground. Saving goes through the system file picker, so it does not request internet, location, contacts, or storage permissions, and it does not require an account.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, crash, or advertising SDKs that send user data off-device.
  • No account identifiers, email, or location collection by the app.
  • No sale or sharing of card data with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
