Privacy Policy — SquirrelSound

Package: works.squirrel.sound · Developer: Squirrel Works · Last updated: 2026-11-01

SquirrelSound is a live microphone spectrum analyzer. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • Microphone audio is used only on your device to draw the spectrum display.
  • The app does not upload, record to the cloud, or share microphone audio.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no sale of personal data.

Data the app processes on-device

With your permission, the app captures short buffers of microphone input, computes a spectrum (FFT) locally, and renders it on screen. Audio samples are not sent to Squirrel Works servers.

Permissions

Microphone permission is required for the spectrum view. You can revoke it in system settings; the analyzer will stop working until permission is granted again.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, crash, or advertising SDKs that send user data off-device.
  • No account identifiers or contact information collection by the app.
  • No sale or sharing of audio data with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
