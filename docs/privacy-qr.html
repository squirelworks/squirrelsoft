Privacy Policy — SquirrelQR

Package: works.squirrel.qr · Developer: Squirrel Works · Last updated: 2026-11-01

SquirrelQR is an on-device QR code scanner. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • Camera frames are used only on your device to detect and decode QR codes.
  • The app does not upload camera images or scan results to our servers.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no sale of personal data.

Data the app processes on-device

With your permission, the app previews the camera and decodes QR payloads locally so you can view or act on the result on the device. Camera imagery and decoded text are not transmitted by the app to Squirrel Works.

Permissions

Camera permission is required to scan. You can revoke it in system settings; scanning will stop until permission is granted again.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, crash, or advertising SDKs that send user data off-device.
  • No account identifiers or contact information collection by the app.
  • No sale or sharing of scan data with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
