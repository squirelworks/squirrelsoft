SquirrelQR 0.1.7 (versionCode 10), following 0.1.6.

## What's new

- **Torch and zoom.** Toggle the torch and pinch or slide zoom through CameraX
  `CameraControl` on the back camera.
- **Batch scan.** Keep the camera running, collect unique codes by content, and
  share the list as plain text or CSV.

## Downloads

- `SquirrelQR-0.1.7-android.apk`: signed Android build (Android 8.0+).
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

There is no desktop build yet. Desktop screen-region and webcam scanning is on the roadmap (S3.4). Play users get the same version through the store.
